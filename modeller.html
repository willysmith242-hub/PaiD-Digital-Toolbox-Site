Important engineering limits

This prototype only creates simple boxes and cylinders from dimensions you enter. It does not interpret photos, hand sketches or PDF drawings, and it does not check strength, pressure ratings, tolerances, standards or fabrication suitability. STL is a mesh, not an editable feature-based CAD file, and the STL format does not store the model's units. Confirm the unit before importing the mesh into CAD software. The editable JSON export does preserve the selected unit. Have safety-critical designs reviewed by a qualified engineer.
